THE NATURALS

review

The Naturals is an extremely gripping, exhilarating series of books following 17 year old Cassandra Hobbes, reeling from her psychic mother's murder, who teams up with the FBI Youth program called "The Naturals Program", alongside 4 other teenagers who were "naturals" at spotting lies, reading emotions, mathematical efficency and analyzing behavioral patterns- like Cassie does. But, this isn't like any other youth program. They've been recruited to specifically solve cold cases- murder cases that could never be solved. As Cassie settles into the program, making friends, she alongside her team uncover a series of chilling hints and clues that not only help aid active murder cases, but help Cassie recognize a pattern with the murders, and what happened to her mom. Will they be able to crack the cases? Read to find out!

POWERLESS

personal thoughts

I feel like this series is such an addictive read. The whole concept immediately pulled me in, and I love the contrast between Paedyn having to hide who she really is and living in a world where being different can cost you everything. I especially enjoyed the chemistry between Paedyn and Kai. Their banter, tension and little moments together are honestly some of my favourite parts. I also really like how the story balances romance with action and emotional moments. It's dramatic and the kind of book that makes you say you'll read just one more chapter and suddenly realise you've read half the book. I do think some parts follow familiar romantasy tropes, but personally, I still enjoyed reading it

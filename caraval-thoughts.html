CARAVAL

personal thoughts

I genuinely think Stephanie Garber has such a beautiful imagination because the world of Caraval is unlike anything else. The descriptions are so dreamy and colourful, and I love how everything feels mysterious, almost like you're walking through a magical dream. I especially enjoyed the atmosphere and the constant feeling that something isn't quite right. The plot keeps you guessing, and I love how the books make you question what is real and what isn't. However, I will say that sometimes the descriptions can get a little too much, and I occasionally wished the story would move forward a bit fast paced instead of spending so much time building the atmosphere. Some twists can also feel a little predictable once you get used to the writing. But overall, it's such an enjoyable and enchanting series, and I think the experience of reading it is what makes it special.

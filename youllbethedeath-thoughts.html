YOU'LL BE THE DEATH OF ME

mystery / crime · personal thoughts

this book sat on my tbr for such a long time, and i can honestly say i do not regret finally reading it. the drama kicks in almost immediately, barely thirty pages in, and i loved watching it unfold. there was never a slow stretch where i felt the urge to skim, because the story just kept pulling me forward. ivy and mateo are genuinely so cute together, and i'm proud to say i shipped them from the very beginning. their chemistry made the high-stakes moments hit even harder, and every time they shared a scene i found myself smiling in the middle of a murder mystery. what i loved most was how many small side stories were woven through the book. each one felt like a tiny puzzle piece, and watching them slowly click together into the main story was so satisfying. nothing felt wasted, and the payoff made me want to flip back and spot every clue i had missed. a fun, addictive and twisty read that more than lived up to the wait.

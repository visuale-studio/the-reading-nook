POWERLESS

review

Powerless is set in a kingdom where people with extraordinary abilities are considered powerful and those without them are treated as weak. Paedyn Gray is an ordinary girl living in a world that has no place for someone like her. After being pulled into the deadly Purging Trials, she finds herself surrounded by danger, secrets and people who could expose her biggest secret. Along the way, she crosses paths with Kai azer, a prince who complicates everything. The series explores survival, loyalty, love and what it really means to be powerful.

SHATTER ME

review

Shatter Me is based on Juliette Ferrars, a girl whose touch is dangerous and can hurt or even kill people. In a world controlled by a powerful and cruel government called the Reestablishment, she has spent most of her life isolated and treated like a monster because of her ability. When she is suddenly given a chance to become part of the very system that has hurt her, she has to figure out who she really is and what she wants. It's a story about power, identity, freedom, love and learning to stop being afraid of yourself.

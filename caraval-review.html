CARAVAL

review

Caraval follows Scarlett Dragna, who has always dreamed of attending the legendary Caraval, a magical performance where the audience becomes part of the show and nothing is quite what it seems. When she finally gets the chance to experience it, she finds herself caught up in a mysterious game filled with illusions, secrets and unexpected twists. Alongside her sister Tella, Scarlett has to navigate a world where it's difficult to know what's real and what's simply part of the performance. As the series continues, the story expands into an even bigger world of magic, secrets and romance.

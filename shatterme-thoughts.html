SHATTER ME

personal thoughts

Personally, I really enjoyed this series, especially because Juliette's character development is such a big part of the story. Watching her slowly become more confident and learn to stand up for herself was genuinely satisfying. I also love Tahereh Mafi's writing style, It's very poetic, emotional and sometimes almost feels like reading someone's private thoughts. And the romantic aspect definitely makes the series even more addictive. However, I do think the pacing can get a little slow in certain parts, and some of the relationship drama feels unnecessarily stretched out. There are also moments when the characters' decisions can be a little frustrating. Still, I think the emotions, character development and overall concept make it a series worth reading.

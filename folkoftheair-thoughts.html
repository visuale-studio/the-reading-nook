FOLK OF THE AIR

personal thoughts

Honestly, I feel like this series has such a special kind of magic to it. I love how Jude has a unique personality, she’s ambitious, stubborn, clever and sometimes makes questionable decisions, but that's exactly what makes her so interesting. And Cardan? I feel the tension between him and Jude is one of the best parts of the entire series. I love how their relationship develops without taking away from the actual storyline. The world-building, the mind games and all the little twists make it so hard to put down. It's the kind of series that stays in your head even after you've finished reading it!

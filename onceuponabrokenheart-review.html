rom com · review

Once Upon a Broken Heart follows Evangeline Fox, a girl who believes deeply in love and happily-ever-afters. When she makes a deal with the mysterious Prince of Hearts, Jacks, she gets pulled into a world of magic, curses, dangerous bargains and unexpected feelings. What starts as an attempt to fix a broken heart slowly turns into something much bigger. The series feels like stepping into a fairytale, except nothing is ever as perfect or simple as it seems.

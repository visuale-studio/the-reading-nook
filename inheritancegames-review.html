INHERITANCE GAMES

review

The Inheritance Games is a delicious, tumultous series of mystery, romance, and unexpected twists. Centered around 17 year old Avery Kylie Grambs, who was generally just another ordinary girl, still recovering from the untimely death of her mother, finds herself the unexpected heir to a billion dollar fortune left to her by Tobias Hawthorne, a Texan Billionare, with 4 grandsons in line to the inheritance- but only upon meeting a certain condition. She must figure out how and why she was chosen to be heir to the fortune- all the while forging connections, and ensuring she remains safe.

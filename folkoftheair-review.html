THE FOLK OF THE AIR

review

The Folk of the Air follows Jude Duarte, a human girl living in the dangerous and magical world of Faerie, where humans are looked down upon and power means absolutely everything. Jude wants to prove that she belongs, but getting involved in the politics, secrets and power struggles of Faerie is far more dangerous than she expects. At the centre of it all is Cardan, a cruel, arrogant faerie prince who makes her life anything but easy. It's a story filled with betrayal, ambition, complicated relationships and court politics.

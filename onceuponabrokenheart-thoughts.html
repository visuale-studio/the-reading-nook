ONCE UPON A BROKEN HEART

rom com · personal thoughts

I absolutely adore the atmosphere of this series. There's something about the writing that makes everything feel dreamy, magical and almost unreal. I personally love books that make me feel like I'm living inside a fairytale, and this series does exactly that. Evangeline is such a hopeful character, and I find that really endearing, especially when everything around her is so uncertain. And Jacks is honestly such an interesting character. There's just something about him that makes you want to understand him more and more. The romance, the mystery and the fairytale-like setting make this series such a lovely reading experience. It's the kind of series I'd want to read again just to experience the magic for the first time. I only wished that the plot was slightly more prominent and unpredictable.

KEEPER OF THE LOST CITIES

review

sophie foster, just another regular teenage girl, finds her world turned over when she finds out she is not a human, but rather an elf, after an encounter with a mysterious boy with teal eyes at a museum. After leaving her entire life behind, she wanders into a foreign world, one that she believed could only exist in stories she grew up hearing. The magical, fanastical feel takes a sharp turn once she finds out the story of her creation, and attacks on her. Will she be able to settle in? Read to find out!
